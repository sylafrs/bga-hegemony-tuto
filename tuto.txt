		Welcome to the tutorial for Hegemony: Lead your class to Victory!

		The nation is in disarray. The economy is failing and political tensions are rising. The [red]*working class*[/red] faces a dismantled welfare system, the [blue]*capitalists*[/blue] are losing their profits, the middle class is gradually fading, and the state is sinking into a deep deficit. In these troubling times, the only person who can provide guidance is... *you*!

		Hegemony is an asymmetrical board game for 2 to 4 players, with games that take between 2 and 3 hours on BGA.

	Step #2
	
		In Hegemony, you will play The [red]*Working Class (WC)*[/red], *The Middle Class (MC)*, [blue]*The Capitalist Class (CC)*[/blue] or you will play *the State*.

		Each player has a different set of abilities and their own agenda to fight for.

		In this tutorial, we will play a two-player game as the [red]Working Class[/red], and we will fight some [blue]Capitalists[/blue]!
		
	Step #3
	
		*The Middle Class* appears in 3- and 4-player games.
		*The State* is being controlled only in 4-player games, but is always present in all games.

		But before going into specifics about the classes, let's explain how the game is played overall.
		
	Step #4
	
		Hegemony is played in 5 *Rounds*.

		Each round is divided in multiple *Phases*:
		- The Preparation phase 
		- The Action phase
		- The Production phase
		- The Elections phase
		- The Scoring phase 
		
	Step #5
	
		In the *Preparation phase* we draw cards, move the Round marker, and do everything a Class and the State must do at the beginning of each Round (except for the 1st Round which is replaced by Game Setup).
		
	Step #6
	
		In the *Action phase* is where most of the fun lies.

		We will play cards to interact with the board and other players.

		At the start of this phase, all players have 7 cards.
		
	Step #7
	
		Players take *turns* in order, starting with the [red]Working Class[/red], followed by the Middle Class, the [blue]Capitalist Class[/blue], and then the State.
		
	Step #8
	
		During its turn, a player must play a card either for its effect or to perform a *Basic Action*.

		A player can also take one *Free Action* that they can perform before or after the *Card Action*.

		Once all players have finished their turn, we start again, and again, and again, and again, and *[red]STOP![/red]*

		*We only play 5 turns out of the 7 cards*. The two remaining cards are kept for next round.
		
	Step #9
	
		Next is the *Production Phase*: Workers work at their workplaces, in exchange for money, and this produces goods and services for the workplace owner.

		Then the Population needs to be fed, and the State needs to exto-- I mean, collect taxes to avoid bankruptcy.
		
	Step #10
	
		Like with the Preparation and Scoring phases, almost everything is handled by BGA but players may have choices to make.

		(For example: if the Population needs more food than you currently have, whom shall you buy it from?)
		
	Step #11
	
		After the production comes the time for elections!
		It's the *Elections Phase*.

		For each policy that has a proposed bill, an election is carried out in order, starting with the fiscal policy to the immigration policy.

		..but we will see how elections work later.
		There will be cubes. Lots of them.
		
	Step #12
	
		The Round concludes with a nice little phase where nothing bad happens: the *Scoring Phase*.

		During this phase, each player earns points according to their board state.

		At the end of Round 5, we will also add the end of game points and the game will be over, a class will rise to victory... May it be yours.

		How much shall we make the classes pay?

	Step #39
	
		Then come *4 and 5: The Welfare State policies.*

		It declares how much the services the state offers actually cost.

		- [red]*Socialism* (A)[/red]: they cost nothing to the buyer, but we add 2 times the welfare tax multiplier to the tax multiplier

		- *Balanced* (B): they cost 5 Vardis, and we add the welfare tax multiplier to the tax multiplier

		- [blue]*Neo-liberalism* (C)[/blue]: they cost 10 Vardis, and tax multiplier is unaffected.

		When those change, The [blue]*Capitalist*[/blue] and *Middle* classes can change the prices of their Goods and Services.

		[blue][tip] For example, if 3A, 4A and 5A are applied, then the tax multiplier would be : 3 + 2x2 + 2x2 = 11 : the maximum value.

		[tip] But if we have 3C, 4A and 5A : then tax modifier would be only 1 + 2x0 + 2x0 = 1 : the minimum value. (those policies will likely end up in a State bankrupcy)[/blue]

		The Trade Unions slots are specialized and workers placed there are always commited.

		They have several uses..
		
	Step #92
	
		Operating Trade Union benefits:

		*At Production Phase*, the Working class gains 1 Influence per "operating" Trade Union

		*At Scoring Phase*, the Working class gains 2 VP per "operating" Trade Union

		*During demonstrations*, if at Production phase the demonstration is still in effect, "operating" Trade Unions increase the amount of VP your opponents will lose.

	Step #103
	
		This is almost the end : let's quickly talk about the *Middle Class* (3/4 players) and The *State* as a player (4 players).
		
	Step #104
	
		The *Middle Class* is a mix of the [red]Working Class[/red] and the [blue]Capitalist Class[/blue]. They want the laws to be Balanced.

		Like the [red]Working class[/red] they have workers, a prosperity, can buy goods and services.

		And like the [blue]Capitalist class[/blue] they have some companies on their own.

		[img]https://snipboard.io/9ediRJ.jpg[/img]
		
	Step #105
	
		The *Middle Class* don't have Business Deals, Trade Unions, Strikes or Demonstrations at their disposal but they can sell to the Foreign Market.

		Instead they can buy the Good and Services they produce for free, and they can force their workers to do an Extra Shift on one of their company as a Base Action.

		Moreover, they earn victory points for each transaction when they export goods.

		During Election Phase, they add cubes equal to the higher value between :
		- Half the population (like the [red]Working Class[/red])
		- Half the number of operationnal companies (like the [blue]Capitalist Class[/blue])
		
	Step #106
	
		Finally their companies can be inactive, operated, or fully operated.

		A *fully operated company* is a company with at least *2 operating workers*.

		At Preparation Phase, they lose 2 Prosperity instead of 1.

		But at Scoring Phase, if their Prosperity value is lower than the count of their *fully operated companies*, they earn +1 Prosperity.
		
	Step #107
	
		Some companies can be operated with only 1 Middle Class worker, and can be "upgraded" when a [red]Working Class[/red] worker is also affected.

		[img]https://snipboard.io/QbUAxC.jpg[/img]

		This is the only case in which Middle class workers and Working class workers are working in the same company. 
		(and the only case in which we can have workers and empty slots)

		Otherwise, the companies *must* be empty or filled with the same type of worker.
